# Council question, 9 Oct 2026 (framed neutrally for the five advisors)

## The decision
Is the approach we have built over the last two days the right way to launch and grow userandproduct.com, and where will it fail? "The approach" means all of it together: the research method, the data-driven topic tree, the simplified site tree, the intent-first rule, the five sections (Articles, Useful links, Books, Tools, Templates), the launch waves, and the writing cadence.

## Who and why
The owner has 15 years in product design and product management and is the only author. Goals, in order: authority in both directions (the experience backs the site, the site backs the owner); organic search traffic; later, books with affiliate links, a tools and links directory, calculators, templates, courses. The site should read like Smashing Magazine (practitioner magazine), not NN/g, and cover UX, product management and the business side together. Audience: designers, product managers, researchers, leaders, analysts, agile people; secondarily founders, students, career switchers. Sustainable cadence: about three substantial pieces a week from the owner, with Claude doing research, briefs, outlines, fact checks and edits.

## What was done (8 to 9 Oct 2026, $3.55 of a $5 cap)
- Phase 0: 78 Google first pages across the five sections; 51 competitor sitemaps; 12 community tool lists.
- Phase 1: keyword harvest (a noisy first attempt via a category endpoint, then a phrase-match re-run), 3,255 on-topic phrases, manual review of 6,587 unassigned phrases (204 recovered).
- Phase 2: competitor traffic for 96 domains; ranked keywords for the four strongest.
- Phase 3: section demand and stock (what directories, book lists and template galleries list).
- Phase 4: Google Ads volumes, five-year Trends, page-one SERP per topic, a 0-100 score (demand 25, difficulty 20, intent 15, soft shelf 10, no answer box 10, trend 10, few competitors 10).
- Intent pass: every phrase labelled (define, learn, compare, template, tool, question, career, brand, dictionary) and field-fit (yes/partial/no); career, brand, dictionary and site-lookup demand removed before scoring; article capacity per unit.
- An AI harvest (16 seeds): AI phrased with the field is a small search topic (44 phrases).

## What the data said
- Of 4.33M monthly searches in the clustered phrases, 2.45M are really ours. 67% of demand is "what is X" definitional searches; practitioner intent (how-to, compare, template, tool) is small.
- Every head term has a Google AI Overview. Only 6 of 68 head terms have a soft page one.
- Page one is held by Reddit, LinkedIn, SaaS vendors (ProductPlan, Atlassian) and big generalists (Coursera, Wikipedia). The magazines the site models on barely appear and are small by traffic (NN/g about 63K visits a month, Smashing about 17K).
- Books: Reddit and Amazon on 11 of 12 pages; personal practitioner lists outrank publications. Templates: Notion, Figma, ClickUp galleries. Tools: independent calculator sites; four calculator head terms mean something else (cooking rice, medical ICE, mortgages). Links: Reddit on 13 of 16 pages.
- Template demand runs through product topics (user stories 41% template searches, roadmaps 34%, PRDs 33%).
- Metrics phrases barely exist ("ux metrics", "product metrics"); people search "kpi examples", "retention rate".
- 45 of 68 head terms rising over five years.

## The structure decided
- Reader first, SEO second. Three domains (Design, Product, Business) as menu groupings with a hub page each. Thirteen categories hold the articles: UX research, UX design, UI design, Design systems, Accessibility; Product management, Product strategy, Product discovery, Project management (owner undecided); Product metrics, Growth and pricing, Product marketing, Leadership and teams. No second-level categories: 49 article groups are headings on a category page. About 210 articles possible across the tree. Each category has a plain-language hover card with score, difficulty, intent mix.
- Launch proposal (Wave 1, from the data): UX research, product strategy, product management, requirements and PRDs, user stories, AI as one explainer; 81 articles, about six months at three a week. Waves 2 to 4 cover the rest of the year. The owner has not yet given fit ratings or chosen the launch size.
- Sections: Useful links (directory, categories by domain and job), Books (curated list, owner-written), Tools (calculators such as sample size, RICE), Templates (user story, PRD, roadmap, persona). Stack: WordPress, GeneratePress child theme, a structure plugin, Rank Math, plain CSS, no build step.

## What is at stake
Six to twelve months of the owner's writing time at three pieces a week; the owner's professional credibility (the site is meant to back their name); a small budget. A wrong structure or a wrong launch set is expensive to unwind once URLs and categories are live.
