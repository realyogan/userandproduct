Three domains, eleven categories

Each category shows how easy it should be to rank, with the number of articles it can carry on the right. Groups sit beneath it. Hover over a line, focus it or tap it for its numbers and everything that was merged into it.

  • Easy Weak pages on Google's first page and a high score
  • Promising Low difficulty with a fair score, or a high score with some competition
  • Takes work Real competition or a modest score: it needs strong pages and time
  • Hard Strong sites hold Google's first page; expect a long climb
  • Parked Most of its searches are not our readers, so it waits
  • No data No difficulty figure, so we cannot say how hard it is yet

The rule, from the median difficulty (0 to 100) and the score (0 to 100): difficulty 35 or more is hard; under 15 with a score of 60 or more is easy; under 15 with 45 to 59, or 15 to 29 with 60 or more, is promising; anything else is takes work. Parked and no-difficulty lines are grey whatever their numbers.
