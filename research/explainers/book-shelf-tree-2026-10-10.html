This is every book on the shortlist, placed in the category where it would sit on the site. Open a category to see its books. Hover over a title, or tap it, to see what the book is for, who it suits, which reading lists back it and what articles it could seed.

Each book carries one of three tags. The canon is the set nearly every reading list names. Widely recommended books show up on many lists. Good but niche books are strong in one narrow corner. A dashed aged mark means parts of the book have dated, though the core still holds, and 2 homes means it could sit in a second category.
