Logo concepts, round 1

Twelve directions for userandproduct (one word, as the domain), 9 October 2026. A to G came first; H to L each start from a different root: typography history, the user's eye, evidence, architecture and the printed page. Every letter is drawn as a path on one geometric grid; a final typeface can replace it later. Card view with size ramps: preview.html.

Accents

Masthead red: #B3141C on white, #F2555C on near-black
Ink blue: #1F3FBF on white, #7D96FF on near-black

Text is #0A0A0C on white and #F4F4F5 on near-black. Each concept uses one accent only.

How to choose

  1. Put the header lockup on the article mockup at 32 to 40px tall, in light and dark. It should look like a publication, not a startup.
  2. Try the mark as a LinkedIn avatar, a 64px circle crop next to your name.
  3. Find the 16px tab among your real open tabs. If it takes more than a second, it fails.
  4. Check the single-colour version: one-colour print, a book spine, a stamp.

A. Wordmark

Pure wordmark

The name is the logo: userandproduct as one heavy lowercase word, with "and" in a light weight and the accent so it reads user / and / product at a glance.

Accent: Ink blue #1F3FBF (on near-black #7D96FF). Favicon: UP in black weight over an accent rule. Files: a-wordmark-icon.svg, a-wordmark-lockup.svg, a-wordmark-stacked.svg, each also as -dark and -mono.

H. Et

Typography history

The ampersand began as a scribe's ligature of "et", Latin for "and". Here the e's bar runs on as the t's crossbar, and the t is in rubric red, the scribe's colour for what matters.

Accent: Masthead red #B3141C (on near-black #F2555C). Favicon: the et ligature alone. Files: h-et-icon.svg, h-et-lockup.svg, each also as -dark and -mono.
