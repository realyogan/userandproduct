Logo concepts, round five

Ten concepts from two starting points. Six read the three-legged structure the owner shared (a spine, two struts, three terminals) with its cross-bar removed and its proportions, strokes, terminals and joints changed until the source is no longer recognizable. Three develop round four's weighted ring, and one joins the two ideas.

Each concept is shown at 560px on its paper and on near-black, then in a 1280px site header strip on white and on near-black, then the mark, the reduced form that survives at 16px, favicon renders, one color, the avatar and a profile tile. Below that, the three closest existing logos found, and the notes. Pin any lockup to compare.

How originality was checked. Logo files and site icons were opened and looked at, not only searched by description: Oyster HR, Carraro, Asana, Neo4j, Atari, Mercedes-Benz, Maserati, Mitsubishi, Target, the power symbol and the peace symbol. Searches also covered the source symbol (a stroke diagram from a Reiki teaching set; the searches could not name it with certainty, and none of the ten keeps its cross, its braces at the original proportion or its open rings at the feet), runes and public marks with a stem and two diagonals (the Yr rune, the broad arrow), tripod, plumb, node and network marks, and discs with an opening or slot.

What changed after the owner's note on look-alikes. Round four's weighted ring is close to Oyster HR's mark, so no plain disc with an opening ships here: Q7 replaces the opening with a slot cut to the center, Q8 makes the ring the foot of the tripod, Q9 is a block with the same slot, and Q10 carries the ring on three legs.

Tripod readings: Q1 plumb line, Q2 stand, Q3 tree of three, Q4 braced tripod, Q5 chandelier, Q6 pure reduction.

Weighted ring, rebuilt: Q7 slotted disc, Q8 weighted foot, Q9 slotted block.

Fusion: Q10 ring on three legs.

RationaleThe structure read as a plumb line: three cords drop from one bare point, each to a weight. The cords are hairlines and the weights are solid and heavy, the middle one larger and lowest, so the eye goes down and the mark settles. Nothing sits above the point. Read it as user, product and the reader held true by one line, or just as a plumb.

Stroke and terminals, type, paletteHairline cords, three solid weights (the center one larger and lower). Fraunces SemiBold, lowercase. Oxblood on Cream.

OriginalityChanged from the source: the cross-bar is gone and nothing rises above the apex; the two short braces between the feet are gone; the open rings are solid weights of two sizes; the proportion is tall and narrow (about 0.6:1) instead of the source's broad triangle; strokes are hairline against heavy discs. Of the source only 'three lines from a point' remains, which is generic geometry.

Reading and 16px formA plumb line with three weights: hairline cords from one point, the heaviest weight lowest. At 16px: one weight on a cord: a plumb bob.

Masthead or pitch deck?Masthead. A hairline and three weights has the quiet of a museum or a science press; it needs Fraunces beside it, and the hairlines drop out below 24px, which is why the favicon is the plumb bob alone.
