# Illustration frame reference (Tiger Data), 9 Oct 2026

Owner shared the SVG of a Tiger Data article illustration ("one run shape") as the model for our
explainer pictures. What it is made of (measured from the file, 1195 x 623):

- A white card with a 1.5px #CCCCD5 border and small chamfered notches cut into each edge
  (two per long side at about one third and two thirds of the height, one per short side near the
  middle), 8 to 9px deep, giving a "cut card" feel.
- A faint dot grid over the card: a 12-unit pattern with a 2x2 dot, scaled 9.6x, at 14% opacity.
- A title top-left in a heavy sans (the article title or the figure's claim), about 34px.
- The brand mark top-right (their tiger disc, about 62px tall); ours will be smaller, about 32px.
- The diagram in the middle: rounded rectangles with 2px #3F3F46 strokes and pale fills (#F4F4F5),
  highlighted ones in tints (#B7CAFF with #2D4A99, #FFDED9 with #CC4636, #F9FFBE with #C1CC4A),
  labels in a monospace, vertical 2px rules as timelines.
- A caption row along the bottom in small monospace: axis values or legend text.

The owner wants: the same frame for our illustrations, our mark small in the corner, shown in the
sample article first; once confirmed, written as a rule so illustrations are reproducible.
