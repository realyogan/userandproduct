# Reference logos: what authority looks like

Collected 2026-10-09 for the feeling board
([../feeling-board.html](../feeling-board.html),
http://localhost/user-and-product/research/mockups/references/feeling-board.html).

**Internal reference only.** These are other companies' trademarks. They are here so we can study how
they work. Never use, trace, adapt or ship any of them on the site, in social images or in print.
"Public domain" on Wikimedia Commons means the drawing is too simple for copyright (or the copyright
has lapsed); the trademark rights are untouched.

robots.txt was checked for every home page before the screenshot (all allow `/` for generic agents;
Mastercard's robots.txt only answers a browser, and allows `/`). One page load at a time with a pause
of at least a second between requests. Home page shots: headless Edge, 1280 x 800, first screen only.
