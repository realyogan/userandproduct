Logo 3: the owner's mark, in color

The owner's own drawing: a disc with a person cut out of it, a rounded diamond for the head over a wide curved band for the shoulders. The same band also reads as a smile or a bowl. The shapes are exactly as drawn; only the color changes below.

  • Signal blue
  • Deep navy
  • Cobalt
  • Near-black ink
  • Warm charcoal
  • Deep green
  • Oxblood
  • Vermilion
  • Plum
  • Teal
  • Mustard with a dark figure
  • Two-tone blue
  • Signal blue to cobalt
  • Navy to teal
  • Plum to vermilion
  • Charcoal to ink

Color options

Twelve options. Each disc is checked against white and against near-black (#111111) for the 3:1 a graphic needs. Where a disc fails on dark, its dark-mode version is a lighter tone of the same color. The cut-outs are true holes, so on any ground they show the ground, except in C11 and C12, where they are filled. Avatars are opaque, like an upload, so the figure in them is white (ink in C11).

What gets exported

The export folder holds the signal blue option (C01) ready to try for real. Every other option has its masters in svg/ and its renders in png/; any of them can be exported the same way once one is chosen.

  • export/favicon.svg: the signal blue disc with empty cut-outs; switches to #8EA2FF in dark themes.
  • export/favicon.ico, favicon-16.png, favicon-32.png, favicon-48.png: the disc with the figure filled white, so it holds in light and dark tabs.
  • export/apple-touch-icon-180.png: opaque full square, signal blue with the white figure; iOS rounds the corners.
  • export/social-square-1080.png, social-square-400.png: the square avatar for Instagram, LinkedIn and X uploads.
  • export/social-circle-1080.png, social-circle-400.png: the disc edge to edge, transparent corners.
  • export/gradient/: each gradient as a 1080 square and circle, dithered against banding; social only.
  • export/head-snippet.html: the three tags for the theme head.
  • svg/mark.svg: the clean master: the owner's geometry exactly, one compound path, the cut-outs true holes.
  • svg/mark-solid.svg: the same with the cut-outs filled white, for photos.
  • svg/mark-black.svg, mark-white.svg, mark-currentcolor.svg: monochrome and inline masters.
  • svg/lockup-black.svg, lockup-white.svg, stacked-black.svg, stacked-white.svg: monochrome lockups.
  • svg/<option>/: per option: mark, mark-dark, single (when the cut-outs are filled), avatar-circle, avatar-rounded, avatar-square, lockup, lockup-dark, stacked, stacked-dark.
  • png/<option>/: per option: favicons at 16 and 32 px on white and near-black, transparent tab icons, the thumbnail tile; for gradients also the avatars as PNG.

Built by build.py in this folder from source/Logo-owner.svg (the owner's Logo.svg is never touched). Checks in check.py. The Instagram, LinkedIn and tab previews are plain HTML mocks, the same for every option. Rebuild notes in README.md.
