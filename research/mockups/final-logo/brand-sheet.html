userandproduct brand sheet

The logo for userandproduct.com: the Signal mark (four tiles of two sizes, turned 45 degrees) and the name set in Inter Display Bold. Decided 2026-10-09. Every file here is built by build.py.

Wordmark

Typeface
Inter Display (Rasmus Andersson and contributors)
Weight
Bold (700)
Case
All lowercase, one word: userandproduct
Tracking
-25 (thousandths of an em), set with the font's kerning
Lockup
Mark 1.4 cap heights tall, centred on half the cap height; gap 0.38 cap heights
Files
Outlined as paths; no live text, so nothing depends on a font loading
Licence
Inter is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The OFL allows logos made from its outlines; no credit is required. The outlines are not exclusive: anyone can set the same word in the same face.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space, x, is the height of one small tile (about 29 percent of the mark's height). Keep at least x empty on every side. At a 30 pixel tall lockup, x is about 7.7 pixels.

Mark
16 pixels minimum (use the favicon cut at 16 to 48 pixels)
Lockup
120 pixels wide minimum; below that, use the mark alone

Do and don't

Do

  • Use the files as supplied: light, dark, black or white.
  • Pick light or dark by the background, not by taste.
  • Scale proportionally and keep the clear space.
  • Use the white version on photographs, over a calm area.

Don't

  • Recolour beyond the four versions.
  • Stretch, squash, rotate or re-space the logo.
  • Outline it, or add shadows, glows, gradients or other effects.
  • Colour the tiles in more than one colour.
  • Retype the name in another font or in capitals.
