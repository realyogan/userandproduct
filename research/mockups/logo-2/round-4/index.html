Round 4: refining the shortlist, and fresh marks

What the round found. Cup and ball: 45 degrees holds the three readings best (R4-04: a U on its side, a head with a raised arm, the straight cut edge as the stem of a D); lifting the ball (R4-08) makes the person read first at the cost of the D. Speech mark: the long tails fix the cricket ball. The heavier tail (R4-12) survives smallest and still reads as a quote mark at 16 px; the longer, finer tail (R4-11) is as clear at 32 px. The wedge (R4-14) reads at any size, but as a chat bubble.

The brief

  1. Refine the four the owner shortlisted: Cup and ball, Speech mark, Square coin, Shelf.
  2. Cup and ball tilted to the left at 30, 45 and 60 degrees, free and in a disc, and with the ball lifted.
  3. Speech mark: make the tail the drawing, tested at 32 px and 16 px first.
  4. Square coin and Shelf: small variations so the exact proportion can be picked.
  5. New ground: figurative candidates from round 3 and eight fresh marks, at the standard of one clean idea cut from a solid form.

Numbering is fixed once the owner has seen this board: new marks go at the end, dropped marks stay and are marked as dropped.

Still out

  • Rockets, folders, switches, bookmarks, cursors, keys, pens.
  • A profile head in a disc, and anything from the old exploration (plumb lines, keystones, lecterns, true north were tried there).
  • Letters as the main idea: only the Square coin family is letter-based.
