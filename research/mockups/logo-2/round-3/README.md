## The brief for this round

Three groups. A: the coin evolved (the container and the mark are one object). B: the orbit evolved (two forms
meeting, the fold where they meet). C: fresh. Every mark: authority and trust first, mass, one idea, a two-tone
fold, an app-icon feel, legible at 32 px, blue by default with four in an alternative color. Wordmark: Inter
Display Bold.

Owner direction during the round: not everything should be U, D and letters. The standard for cleanness is a
solid disc with one sharp figurative silhouette cut from it, one idea, no fuss (a reference for the level, never a
shape to copy; a profile head in a disc was already tried in the old exploration and is not repeated). Group C
became mostly figurative cut-outs, and the letter-based marks in A were trimmed so letters are a minority.

## The board, per mark

1. Avatars: 110 px circle and rounded square, on white and near-black.
2. Monochrome at 160 px: white on black, black on white, and one color with no fold.
3. The color mark at 160 px on both grounds.
4. Favicons: rendered 32 px PNGs in color (white and near-black grounds) and in monochrome, each enlarged three times.
5. Previews: an Instagram profile (110 px circle avatar, username, three counters, a bio line), a LinkedIn company
   page (the square logo at 100 px on a banner strip in the mark's container color), and a browser tab strip in
   light and dark with the 16 px favicon. The mocks are plain HTML and CSS on the shared tokens, the same for every
   mark, with no real platform assets.
6. The thumbnail: lockup 120 px wide in the corner of a 600 x 338 tile.
7. The header lockup at 236 px.
8. The words: the idea, why "oh nice", what it means (two to four plain sentences in the house voice), the
   authority check, the construction and the nearest existing logos.
