# Books shelf: candidate list (10 Oct 2026)

A wide first pass at the books a product person, designer or founder would expect on the userandproduct.com Books shelf. There are 181 books. Each sits on one of the thirteen site categories or on one of the brief's extra shelves (psychology and behavioral science, business and entrepreneurship, personal and professional development); the brief's marketing and leadership shelves map to Growth and pricing, Product marketing and Leadership and teams.

Tiers: **A** is the canon everyone names; **B** is widely recommended; **C** is good but niche. The list is above the expected 80 to 150 on purpose (no cap was set): the C tier and the softer B picks are where to trim. Years are first publication; the edition column names a notable later revision. Titles, authors and years were checked against Open Library for every book and against publisher or catalog pages where an edition or year was in doubt (see Sources used). All descriptions are our own words. The same data, with a few extra fields (site domain, brief shelf, article group, list backing), is in `candidates-2026-10-10.csv`.
