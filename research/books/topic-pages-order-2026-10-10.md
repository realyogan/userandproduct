# Book topic pages: order, titles, metas

Decided inputs (10 October 2026): the Books menu opens one shelf page (an intro in the owner's voice, then tiles with a
thumbnail and a title, one per topic page); each topic page holds its books as sections; ten topic pages at launch,
then two a month until all sixteen are live; no folding of topics. Demand and page-one data:
`research/seo/keywords/book-topics-summary-2026-10-10.md`. Book stock from `candidates-2026-10-10.csv` (tier A plus B).

Order weighs four things: demand, how open page one is, fit with the site's subject, and how many good books we hold.

## Title pattern

Owner's rule (10 October 2026): titles and descriptions are based on keywords
(`research/seo/keywords/book-topics-2026-10-10.csv`). The title tag leads with the strongest searched phrase for the
topic ("best <topic> books", "<topic> books", or "books for <audience>" where that is what people search: books for
entrepreneurs 1,900, books for new managers 260, books for product managers 590). A second searched phrase or the
page's honest angle follows only if the whole tag stays at 60 characters or fewer. Audience tails nobody searches
("for product teams", "for designers") are dropped. The meta description (about 140 to 155 characters) works in the
main phrase and one secondary searched phrase naturally, says who the page is for and what it promises, and is unique
per page. The H1 is the plain name using the main phrase. Site name once at the end of the title tag (not counted in
the character counts). Where Google reports one number for a group of near-identical phrases, "same count" marks the
second phrase: it adds wording, not new searches.

## The shelf page

Menu "Books" opens `/books/`: a short intro in the owner's voice (who the shelf is for, how it is grouped, that where a
book is dated the page says so), then the tiles, one per topic page, each a code-drawn thumbnail in the house style
with the topic name and the count of books, grouped Design, Product, Business. No books listed directly on the shelf
page; the tiles are the way in. The category hubs show that category's top three books and link to the topic page.
