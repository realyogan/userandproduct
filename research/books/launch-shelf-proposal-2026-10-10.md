# Launch shelf proposal: 40 books

Proposed on 10 October 2026 from research/books/candidates-2026-10-10.csv for the owner to adjust. Weighted to the
three main shelves (Design 12, Product 14, Business 11) with 3 supporting books. Marked "page" = gets its own book
page at launch (13); the rest are shelf entries until they earn a page.

## Design (12)
- UX research: Just Enough Research (page); Interviewing Users; Rocket Surgery Made Easy
- UX design: The Design of Everyday Things (page); Don't Make Me Think (page); Lean UX; Information Architecture: For
  the Web and Beyond; Strategic Writing for UX
- UI design: Refactoring UI (page)
- Design systems: Atomic Design
- Accessibility: Inclusive Design Patterns (the canon is thin; no tier-A book exists)
- (UI design and accessibility are thin; our own pieces can fill them)

## Product (14)
- Product management: Inspired (page); Empowered; Escaping the Build Trap (page); Working Backwards
- Product strategy: Good Strategy Bad Strategy (page); Playing to Win; Outcomes Over Output
- Product discovery: Continuous Discovery Habits (page); The Mom Test (page); Sprint; User Story Mapping; Competing
  Against Luck
- Project management: Shape Up; Accelerate

## Business (11)
- Product metrics: Measure What Matters; Lean Analytics (dated benchmarks, say so)
- Growth and pricing: Monetizing Innovation (page); Hacking Growth
- Product marketing: Obviously Awesome (page); Crossing the Chasm; Made to Stick
- Leadership and teams: High Output Management (page); The Making of a Manager; Radical Candor

## Also on the shelf (3)
- Psychology: Thinking, Fast and Slow (page; note the studies that did not replicate)
- Business and entrepreneurship: The Lean Startup; The Innovator's Dilemma

## Left out on purpose
Personal productivity (Deep Work, Atomic Habits, Getting Things Done, 7 Habits): popular but off the site's subject
and four of the single-source tier-A books; add later if the shelf grows. Business Model Generation, Hooked, Thinking
with Type, Nudge: good, next in line for the weekly drip.
