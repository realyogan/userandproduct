# Topic tree v1.2: Articles, plus the section trees with scores (9 Oct 2026)

The data-driven topic tree for userandproduct.com with meaning as the first test (Phase 4c). A topic is on the
tree because of what people mean when they search, and only then because of how many. Same units, keywords, SERPs
and Trends as `research/seo/topic-tree-v1.1.md`. Factual draft; the owner decides, and owner_fit is still empty.

## What changed from v1.1: intent

- **Meaning per phrase.** Every keyword carries a fine intent (career, brand, dictionary, tool, template, compare,
  learn, define, question, other) and a field fit (yes, partial, no), next to the source label. Rules in
  `research/seo/tools/harvest.py`; the 30 biggest phrases of every unit were read by hand
  (`research/seo/keywords/field-fit-review-2026-10-09.csv`, 385 of 1,816 phrases changed).
- **Only our readers count.** Demand is the variant-adjusted volume of the surviving phrases (field fit yes, or
  partial at half weight; not career, brand, dictionary or site lookup). Across the assigned keywords that is 2,450,485
  of 4,326,060. The intent component is the practitioner share of the surviving phrases. A median KD of 0 (no figure)
  scores 0.5 for every unit (this fixes the UX writing artifact).
- **Article capacity decides pillars.** Each unit counts its distinct practical phrase groups (how-to, comparison,
  template, tool and question phrases at 100+ searches; word-order variants merged). A topic needs 5 or more to be
  a pillar; thinner topics are single-article topics or fold into a parent. A unit where half or more of the
  volume is dictionary, career, brand or another world is parked.
- **What moved.** Prioritization (general) falls from 74.2 to 55.1 and is parked (2,390 of 153,570 searches survive);
  the product part is prioritization frameworks, folded into product strategy. Analytics, metrics (general),
  retention (general), accessibility definitions and task prioritization are parked. Product roles is first by
  score (78.6) but has 4 practical phrase groups, so it folds into product management (discipline). UX writing
  falls from 65.7 to 51.9 with the difficulty fix. Details in `research/seo/keywords/phase4c-summary-2026-10-09.md`.
- **Shortlist movement.** The top eight by score now hold two parked units (analytics, metrics general) and
  several single-article topics, so the launch is chosen by the wave rule in section 3, not by the top eight.

## 1. How to read it and where every number comes from

**Units.** 91 scoring units, unchanged from v1.1 (30 topics, 47 sub-topics, 14 section categories). Units with
head terms and intent: `research/seo/keywords/phase4-units-v1.2-2026-10-09.csv` (`tools/phase4c.py`).

**Per-unit figures** (in `research/seo/keywords/topic-scores-v1.2-2026-10-09.csv`; keyword and page-one evidence in
`topic-evidence-v1.2-2026-10-09.csv`):

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| what it is | one sentence from the surviving phrases: the biggest one and the largest practical phrase groups |
| field demand | variant-adjusted volume of the surviving phrases (partial at half weight); "v1.1 adjusted" is the old figure |
| field share | surviving volume over all volume; dictionary, career, site lookup and another world are shares of raw volume |
| KD | median keyword difficulty of the unit's keywords as in v1.1; 0 (no figure) scores 0.5; the median over surviving phrases with a figure is shown where it differs |
| intent mix | volume share of each fine intent over all the unit's keywords |
| practitioner | learn + compare + template + tool + question over the surviving phrases |
| practical phrase groups | distinct groups of surviving practitioner phrases, word-order variants merged; thin under 5, medium 5 to 15, deep above 15 |
| about N articles | practical phrase groups plus one explainer or hub when surviving definition demand exists; 0 when parked |
| format, section | from the surviving phrases (see `tools/phase4c.py`) |
| Ads, Trends, AI Overview, weak page one, arena | unchanged from v1.1 |

**Score (0 to 100).** Ranked percentiles over all 91 units: demand 25 (field demand), difficulty 20, intent 15
(practitioner share), soft shelf 10, answer box 10, trend 10, competitors 10. owner_fit is not in the score.

**Statuses.** Pillar: 5 or more practical phrase groups across the topic's live parts. Single-article topic:
fewer; it is written as one to a few pieces, or folded into a parent pillar. Parked: half or more of the volume is
dictionary, career, brand or another world.

## 6. Data caveats

- **Meaning by wording is a rule plus a reading.** Rules decide most phrases; only each unit's 30 biggest
  phrases were read by hand. A bare phrase of 1 to 3 words counts as define; 12% of the volume is "other".
- **Practical phrase groups undercount definition articles.** "moscow prioritization" and "technical product
  manager" read as define, so they add one explainer, not a group. Capacity is a floor.
- **Partial counts at half weight.** Generic phrases ("retention rate", "prioritization matrix", sample size
  calculators) count half.
- **Site lookups are the source's label** and some are core phrases ("customer journey map"); they leave demand
  but never park a unit.
- **Books have no fitting intent;** read Books on coverage and authority.
- **Variant inflation, Trends relativity, AI Overview everywhere, rare weak page ones, rule-typed arenas:** as in
  v1.1.
