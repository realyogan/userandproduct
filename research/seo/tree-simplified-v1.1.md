# Simplified site tree v1.1 (9 Oct 2026)

Readers see three domains and thirteen categories. Everything under a category is a heading on that category's page and a label in our plan, not a page of its own. The site has one navigable level: the three domains are menu groupings with one hub page each; the thirteen categories are where articles live; the article groups are not a second level of categories. A group that grows past five or six articles may earn a curated reading-list page later, by hand.

URLs are /category/article/, domain hubs at /design/, /product/, /business/; the taxonomy holds domain terms with category children and an article attaches to the category.

The research tree's 77 article nodes (30 topics, 47 sub-topics) become 13 categories and 61 groups, with the same 210 articles possible. Domain > category > article group, where a group is a heading on the category page, not a navigable category.

Numbers per line: articles possible (the v1.2 "about N articles" estimate, the same 210 as the report; parked units count 0); searches that are ours (field demand); difficulty (volume-weighted median of the parts' median difficulty, rounded); score range (min to max of the live units' scores); band (the report's rule on that difficulty and the top score: KD 35+ hard; under 15 with score 60+ easy; under 15 with 45 to 59, or 15 to 29 with 60+, promising; else takes work; parked and no-difficulty grey). Topic totals (roll-ups) are mapped to a group but counted through their sub-topics, so nothing is counted twice. Where the brief splits one unit across groups, articles and their phrase volume are moved (listed at the end).

Mapping: `research/seo/tree-simplified-v1.1-2026-10-09.csv`. Totals: `research/seo/tree-simplified-v1.1-summary-2026-10-09.csv`. Page: `research/explainers/tree-simplified-2026-10-09.html`.
