## What ranks: directories, vendor listicles, or publication roundups?

- Tool keywords (ux tools, best ux design tools, ux research tools, product analytics tools, roadmap tools,
  prototyping tools, ai tools for product managers, ai tools for designers): mostly SaaS vendor listicles that
  rank their own product first (maze.co, looppanel.com, convert.com, uxcam.com, uxpilot.ai, lyssna.com,
  useberry.com, dovetail.com, canny.io, pendo.io, userpilot.com, builder.io, onehorizon.ai, flowstep.ai,
  bilt.me, banani.co, pixflow.net), plus Reddit and Medium posts. Independent publication roundups are rare
  (uxmastery.com from 2012, careerkarma.com from 2020, techrepublic.com, thedigitalprojectmanager.com).
- Resource keywords (design resources, free design resources, icon libraries, free fonts websites, design
  inspiration websites): mostly directories and asset sites (designresourc.es, toools.design,
  freedesignresources.net, resourceboy.com, flaticon.com, fontawesome.com, cufonfonts.com, 1001fonts.com,
  awwwards.com), with forums and Medium filling the gaps.
- "ux resources" is the odd one out: course and learning pages (udacity.com, talentlms.com, uxpin.com) plus UX
  Planet and Medium; no strong curated directory ranks.

## Anything odd

- "product management tools" and "best product management software" are polluted by project management
  intent (tech.co, themuse.com, softwareadvice.com agile page, apm.org.uk, projectmanagement.com,
  coschedule.com, larksuite.com) and a PIM review page (omr.com). The SERP has not settled on product
  management tooling, so a clear PM tools page could stand out. Both carry paid ads (prodpad.com,
  planview.com); "product analytics tools" also has an ad (growthbook.io).
- "product management tools" ranks a mirrored PDF on governance.ucci.edu.ky at position 5, which looks like
  spam.
- "free fonts websites" and "free design resources" rank thin, deep pages: paginated collections
  (cufonfonts.com collection page 2, fonts4free.net page 14, graphicpear.com page 2, magnific.com page 9), a
  tag page (freedesignresources.net/tag/ppt), a Spanish Pinterest pin, a Tumblr post and a Font Awesome icon
  search URL. Weak quality there means room for a well-built page.
- "design resources" puts an organic result (designresourc.es) above the AI Overview at position 1.
- No featured snippets on any of the 16 keywords.
- Most vendor listicles are dated 2025 or 2026; several older pages still rank (uxmastery.com 2012,
  ux.stackexchange.com 2010, sitepoint.com 2016, blog.logrocket.com 2020).
