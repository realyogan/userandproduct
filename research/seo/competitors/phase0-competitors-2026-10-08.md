# Phase 0 competitors by section, 2026-10-08

Source: 78 Google US desktop SERPs (page one, organic results only), collected on the DataForSEO standard
queue and distilled by `research/seo/tools/serp_distill.py` into `research/seo/serps/page-one-2026-10-08.csv`
(647 organic rows) and `research/seo/serps/features-2026-10-08.csv` (78 rows). Keyword counts per section:
articles 24, links 16, books 12, tools 12, templates 14.

Per-section tables: `research/seo/competitors/<section>-competitors.csv` (every domain with 2 or more page-one
results in that section, plus every seed-list domain that appears at all). Count = organic page-one results
(a domain can hold two positions on one keyword). Domains are exact hosts with "www." removed, so Medium author
subdomains are counted separately from medium.com. Type is one word per domain: publication, expert, saas, forum,
platform, retailer, catalog, directory, gallery, calculator, video, encyclopedia, education, other. Template
galleries run by software vendors (Notion, Figma, Miro, Mural) are typed saas.
